Firefly Grove Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 29, 2026

Firefly Grove is developed and operated by Kottok Technologies. This policy explains how the Android game, its online leaderboards, optional Google Play Games cloud saves, and this policy page handle information.

Information on your device

The game stores a randomly generated player identifier and username, puzzle seeds and board history, your current board and notes, lives, tools, completed levels, times, personal bests, preferences, and pending score submissions on your device. Online profiles also have a private authentication credential stored on the device. These records let you resume play and keep the same player profile. Your username can be changed in Settings.

Online leaderboards

When the configured game connects to our leaderboard service, it can register an online profile automatically. Our AWS service receives a generated player identifier and username. Online races send attempt identifiers, dates, timing information and attempted firefly cells so the server can validate the result. Journey completions send the level, puzzle seed, completion information, remaining hearts and the finds needed to validate the score. We use this information to maintain rankings, prevent duplicate rewards, authenticate requests and detect invalid submissions.

Your username, a short player tag, rank, race result or heart total, and relevant completion statistics may be publicly visible on leaderboards. Please avoid using your real name or other personal information as your username. Private authentication credentials are not displayed publicly; the server stores a hash of the credential.

Google Play Games and optional cloud saves

The Android app includes Google Play Games Services. In a configured build, Google may authenticate an existing Play Games profile when the app starts. Automatic profile-creation prompts are suppressed; you can initiate linking in Settings. Authentication alone does not enable our cloud backup. Google processes its own account, gamer identity, analytics and diagnostic information as described in its policies.

If you choose Link Google Play and save, the game accesses your Play Games player ID and display name to identify the linked account. A private Saved Games snapshot is stored through Google Play Games. It contains journey progress, the current puzzle and notes, tools, preferences, daily personal bests and practice progress, and your leaderboard profile and pending heart submissions. The snapshot includes the existing private leaderboard credential so restoring on another device can recover the same online identity. It is not a public leaderboard entry. Active online race tickets are not transferred.

Cloud saving is optional. You can select Stop cloud saves on this device in Settings. This stops future syncing on that device; it does not delete a backup already stored with Google. Device and cloud saves that differ require choosing which complete save to keep. A local recovery copy is retained before a cloud restore replaces device progress.

Google's handling of information is explained in the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play Games account and game-data controls can be used to manage or delete the game's Google-held data.

Technical information and service providers

Our online service is hosted with Amazon Web Services in US East (Northern Virginia), United States. Network requests expose an IP address and ordinary connection information to the hosting provider. Our application uses a SHA-256 hash of the source IP address for short-lived request limits. Operational logs contain request identifiers, routes, status codes, latency and error information; service tracing helps diagnose failures. We use these records to secure and operate the service, not to build advertising profiles.

AWS processes hosted service data for us. Google processes Play Games and Saved Games data as its service provider and under its own terms. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include; our Gmail provider processes that correspondence. Information may be processed outside your country. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect users, investigate abuse or defend legal rights.

Advertising, analytics and device permissions

The current game has no live advertising SDK and no separate developer-installed advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. Optional reward videos are unavailable in the current Android build; browser development previews may simulate a reward. We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Google Play Games may collect its own SDK analytics and diagnostics as described above. If live advertising is introduced, this policy and the relevant disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

The game does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise device location. It uses internet connectivity for online features and vibration for optional feedback. Local browser storage and cached files support saves and offline play; this policy page uses no cookies, tracking pixels, external fonts or analytics scripts.

How long information is kept

  • Device progress and recovery copies remain until replaced, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled, subject to your device's behavior.
  • Online profiles, authentication records and per-level heart-credit receipts currently have no automatic expiry. They remain until deleted following a verified request or a service-maintenance decision.
  • Daily race records and leaderboard entries are scheduled to expire 92 days after their UTC ranking date. Expiry cleanup is asynchronous, so physical deletion may take additional time.
  • Request-limit records are short-lived and expire after their relevant limit windows. Operational logs are configured for 30-day retention. Database recovery backups can contain earlier records for up to 35 days.
  • Google cloud saves remain until replaced or deleted through the available Google account controls. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary legal or security records.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can change your username and preferences in Settings and stop using online features. To remove device-only data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Clearing device data does not delete online leaderboard records or Google cloud saves.

To request deletion of your Firefly Grove online profile and associated leaderboard and completion records, email kottok.technologies@gmail.com with the subject Firefly Grove data deletion. Include your Player ID from Settings and your username, if available. Please do not send passwords or authentication tokens. We may ask for information needed to verify that the profile belongs to you before processing a request.

For complete removal across devices, stop cloud saving on each linked device and remove the game's saved data using Google Play Games or Google account controls as well. An old cloud save or continued use of the app may otherwise restore or create another online profile. Necessary legal or security records may be retained where required; recovery backups age out according to the retention described above. Contact us if you need help identifying your records.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your information, withdraw optional consent, or raise a concern with a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request. Parents and guardians may also contact us about a child's information. The game does not ask players to provide an age, real name or contact information to play.

Security and updates

Online features use HTTPS. Access controls, server-side credential hashing and encrypted AWS database storage help protect hosted data. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We may update this policy as the game changes and will revise the date above. Material new data uses will be accompanied by the appropriate notice or choice.
